* **Event Title:** Gameshow Cloud Architect (Cloud Architect Workshop)
* **Date & Time:** 09:00 - 12:30, June 20, 2026
* **Location:** AWS Vietnam Office, 26th Floor, Bitexco Financial Tower, 02 Hai Trieu St, Dist 1, HCMC
* **Role:** Intern Attendee & Direct Observer

---

### 4.1.1. Event Objectives

- Build an open networking environment, bringing interns in the First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ) program closer together.
- Consolidate and deepen technical expertise in AWS cloud services, infrastructure design principles, and optimal solution architectures (Cloud Architecture Best Practices).
- Sharpen teamwork skills, fast reasoning reflection, and technical scenario problem-solving under time pressure.

### 4.1.2. Key Highlights

- **Competition Format:** Head-to-head knockout tournament featuring 8 teams (5 members per team) competing across elimination rounds to select the top 2 teams for the Grand Finals.
- **Question Structure:** Each match consists of 10 in-depth multiple-choice scenario questions covering AWS infrastructure, service models, and system architecture, increasing in difficulty over time.
- **Tie-breaker Rules:** The team with the highest accumulated score after 10 questions advances. In case of a tie, an 11th tie-breaker question (buzzer-beater) decides the winner.

### 4.1.3. Strategy and Scoring Mechanisms

Points are added or deducted directly depending on correct or incorrect answers. The highlight of the contest lies in the tactical use of two unique wildcard skills:

- **"Safety Play" Skill:** A defensive strategy for complex questions. A correct answer awards 50% points, while an incorrect answer incurs zero penalty points.
- **"Double Down" Skill:** An aggressive strategy for core strength topics. A correct answer doubles earned points, but an incorrect answer deducts double the point value.

### 4.1.4. Key Takeaways (Audience Perspective)

- **Technical Knowledge:** Observing teams solve fundamental to advanced questions helped me audit my own knowledge base and better understand practical AWS service selection.
- **Risk Management Mindset:** The teams' wildcard decisions offered valuable lessons on trade-off analysis (risk vs. reward) in system design.
- **Teamwork Dynamics:** Impressed by the teams' ability to stay calm, actively listen to peers, and rapidly reach consensus under intense pressure.

### 4.1.5. Personal Experiences and Impressions

- **High Content Quality:** The questions reflected real-world engineering challenges, stimulating situational analysis rather than rote memorization.
- **Energetic Atmosphere:** The hall was filled with vibrant enthusiasm, and smooth MC moderation maintained excitement while keeping the tone welcoming.
- **Interactive Engagement:** Q&A sessions and answer explanations from the organizers made audience members feel fully involved and gained actionable insights.
