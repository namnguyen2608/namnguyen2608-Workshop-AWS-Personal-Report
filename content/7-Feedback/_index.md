### General Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**  
The working environment is extremely friendly, inclusive, and open. Members of the FCAJ community are always willing to support whenever I encounter challenges, even outside standard working hours. The office setup is clean and comfortable, fostering deep focus. Adding more informal team-bonding events could further enhance team camaraderie.

**2. Mentor & Team Admin Support**  
My mentor provided thorough guidance, clearly explaining complex concepts and constantly encouraging me to ask questions. The team admin supported onboarding procedures, documentation, and created optimal conditions for smooth work execution. I highly appreciate that my mentor empowered me to troubleshoot issues independently rather than simply providing ready-made answers.

**3. Alignment Between Work Responsibilities and Academic Major**  
Assigned tasks closely matched the core foundations I learned at university while introducing advanced cloud domains I had never previously experienced. This allowed me to reinforce foundational theory while mastering practical industry skills.

**4. Learning & Skill Development Opportunities**  
Throughout the internship, I gained numerous new skills, including project management tool usage, teamwork dynamics, and professional corporate communication. My mentor also shared valuable real-world experiences that helped clarify my future career trajectory.

**5. Culture & Team Spirit**  
The company culture is incredibly positive: mutual respect is paramount, and work is conducted with high dedication alongside an enjoyable spirit. During tight project deadlines, everyone collaborated seamlessly regardless of seniority. This made me feel like an integral part of the team despite being an intern.

**6. Intern Benefits & Policies**  
The company provides internship stipends and flexible time management when necessary. Furthermore, access to internal training workshops represents a major advantage.


### Additional Questions

* **What satisfied you most during your internship?**  
  What satisfied me most was the opportunity to directly work with cutting-edge AWS Cloud technologies (especially Generative AI services like Amazon Bedrock, Bedrock AgentCore, and BDA). Additionally, the dedicated mentorship and professional, open working culture at AWS Vietnam helped me mature significantly in both technical capability and mindset.

* **What areas do you think the company could improve for future intern cohorts?**  
  The program is already structured exceptionally well. However, incorporating an End-to-End System Architecture overview workshop in the first week would help future interns visualize the overall project roadmap and onboard even faster.

* **Would you recommend this internship to your friends? Why?**  
  Definitely **YES**. It is a world-class internship environment for anyone pursuing Cloud Computing and AI. The First Cloud AI Journey program offers not only AWS-standard practical knowledge but also instills international professional work ethics, enabling students to accelerate beyond university academic theory.


### Proposals & Future Desires

* **What suggestions do you have to improve the overall internship experience?**  
  I propose organizing weekly internal Tech Talks / Knowledge Sharing sessions between intern groups so everyone can exchange practical lessons learned across different technology tracks.

* **Would you like to continue participating in this program in the future?**  
  I am **extremely eager** to do so. I hope to have future opportunities to continue collaborating with the First Cloud AI Journey program or apply for full-time positions at AWS Vietnam to further contribute and advance my career.

* **Other Feedback (Open Share):**  
  Sincere thanks to the First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ) organizing committee, mentors, and the entire AWS Vietnam team for providing such a favorable environment and guiding me throughout this meaningful and unforgettable internship journey!
